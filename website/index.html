Questions

What people ask us first.

Is our data safe?

We use business versions of AI services, whose terms say your data isn't used to train their models. Where we can, tools run in your own accounts, so you keep control of the data and the bill. When personal data is involved, we sign a data processing agreement with you.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build, connect and maintain the tools. Your team needs to know their own work, and we teach them the rest.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

It will, sometimes. Every tool we build flags uncertain cases for a person and keeps your team in charge of anything important. Before launch, we test on your real documents and show you the accuracy.

How soon will we see results?

Quick wins usually come in the first week or two. Most automations go live within 2 to 4 weeks.

Will this replace people?

We design tools to take over tasks. The time goes back to your team, for customers, growth and the work that kept getting postponed. We also train the people who use the tools.

Which AI do you use?

Mostly Claude, from Anthropic, because it handles documents and writing reliably. We pick tools to fit the job, and we can work inside Microsoft, Google or AWS environments if your company requires it.

Tell us the task everyone dreads.

Twenty minutes, at no cost. We'll tell you plainly whether AI can help and what it would take.
