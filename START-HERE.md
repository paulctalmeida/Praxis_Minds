# Start Here: Your First 30 Days

The goal for the first 30 days is **not** a perfect company. It is:

1. **A clear offer** that one specific type of business understands in 10 seconds.
2. **Proof**: a website, 2–3 demo projects, and a workshop you can deliver.
3. **Conversations**: 20+ real conversations with business owners.
4. **The first 1–3 paying (or founding) clients.**

Tick boxes as you go (edit this file, or ask Claude to update it for you).

---

## Rule #1: Start narrow, expand later

You want to offer apps, workflows, dashboards, workshops, websites, reports, presentations, and marketing. **All of these are valid, but you can't market all of them to everyone on day one.** Buyers pay for specialists.

So for the first 6 months:

- **Lead with one flagship offer:** the **AI Opportunity Audit**, which leads into **workflow automation / AI app builds**.
- **Use workshops as your door-opener:** they build trust fast and fill your pipeline.
- **Use websites, viability reports, and pitch decks as cash-flow services:** Claude makes them fast to deliver. Sell them to people already in your network, but don't lead your marketing with them.
- **Pick 1–2 industries** where you already know people (see [Vision & Strategy → Choosing your niche](plan/01-vision-and-strategy.md#4-choosing-your-niche)).

## Week 1: Foundation (decide and set up)

### Day 1: Decide
- [ ] Read [01 – Vision & Strategy](plan/01-vision-and-strategy.md) and score candidate niches with the niche scorecard.
- [ ] **Choose 1 primary niche and 1 backup.** Write them in `CLAUDE.md` under "Current focus".
- [ ] Write your **list of 50 contacts**: business owners, managers, ex-colleagues, family friends who run companies, your accountant, local associations. Use a spreadsheet or a free CRM (HubSpot Free).

### Day 2: Set up the company basics
- [ ] Book a call with an **accountant** to choose a legal structure (sole trader vs. limited company), and ask about VAT/tax registration. See [09 – Legal](plan/09-legal-and-compliance.md#1-company-setup).
- [ ] Check that the name and domains are available: `praxisminds.com`, `praxisminds.ai`, your country domain, and social handles (LinkedIn page, Instagram, YouTube).
- [ ] Buy the domain and set up a professional email (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365), e.g. `hello@` and `yourname@`.
- [ ] Open a separate business bank account.

### Day 3: Set up your AI toolkit
- [ ] Subscribe to **Claude Pro** (upgrade to **Max** when you're building daily). See [08 – Tech Stack](plan/08-tech-stack.md).
- [ ] Install **Claude Code**; you'll use it to build websites, apps, and dashboards.
- [ ] Create an **Anthropic API account** (Console) and add a small amount of credit (€20–€50) for building demos.
- [ ] Create Claude **Projects** for: "Praxis Minds – Sales", "Praxis Minds – Content", and "Praxis Minds – Reports". Paste the matching custom instructions from the [Claude Playbook](plan/07-claude-playbook.md#2-set-up-your-claude-projects).

### Day 4: Brand
- [ ] Choose a tagline (options in [01 – Vision & Strategy](plan/01-vision-and-strategy.md#7-brand)).
- [ ] Create a simple logo (Canva or Claude-generated SVG) and pick 2 brand colors and 1 font.
- [ ] Update your personal **LinkedIn profile**: headline, banner, and About section (prompt in the [Claude Playbook](plan/07-claude-playbook.md)). Create the Praxis Minds company page.

### Day 5: Offer and pricing
- [ ] Finalize your **3 launch offers** from [03 – Pricing](plan/03-pricing.md#launch-offers-first-6-months): AI Quick-Start, AI Opportunity Audit, and the Team AI Workshop.
- [ ] Define the **Founding Client Program**: 3–5 clients at a discount in exchange for a testimonial and a case study.

## Week 2: Build your proof

Your first portfolio is **your own business and demos.** Every piece below is also something you'll sell.

- [ ] **Website:** build praxisminds.com with Claude Code (sitemap and copy outline in [05 – Sales & Marketing](plan/05-go-to-market.md#8-the-praxis-minds-website)). This is portfolio piece #1 for the website service.
- [ ] **Demo app #1, "Document → Data":** an AI tool that reads invoices, orders, or forms and extracts the data into a spreadsheet. Nearly every SME has this pain.
- [ ] **Demo app #2, "Company Knowledge Assistant":** a chat assistant that answers questions from a company's own documents (manuals, policies, FAQs).
- [ ] **Demo #3, "Live KPI Dashboard":** a dashboard built from sample sales and operations data, with an AI-written weekly summary.
- [ ] **Sample viability report:** build one for a fictional business (e.g. a café or a small logistics company) with the [viability report template](templates/viability-report-template.md).
- [ ] Record a **2–3 minute screen video** of each demo; you'll use these in sales calls and on LinkedIn.

> Tip: keep demos generic but realistic. Use your chosen niche's vocabulary, so a logistics owner sees *their* documents.

## Week 4: Launch and talk to people

- [ ] **Announce the launch on LinkedIn**: why you started Praxis Minds, who you help, and the founding-client offer.
- [ ] Send **personal messages to your 50 contacts** (scripts in [outreach messages](templates/outreach-messages.md)). The goal is a 20-minute conversation, not a sale.
- [ ] Offer a **free "AI Lunch & Learn"** (45 minutes) to 3 organizations: a business association, chamber of commerce, coworking space, or a friendly company.
- [ ] Contact **3 potential referral partners**: an accountant, an IT services company, and a marketing or web agency (see [partnerships](plan/05-go-to-market.md#5-referral-partnerships)).
- [ ] Hold **at least 10 discovery conversations.**
- [ ] Close **1–3 founding clients** (AI Quick-Start, Audit, or a workshop).
- [ ] Publish **2 LinkedIn posts per week** from now on.

## If you get stuck

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| "I don't know which niche" | Pick the industry where you can name 10 decision-makers personally. Done. |
| "Nobody replies" | Make the ask smaller (a 20-minute chat, or a free checklist) and more personal (mention their business specifically). |
| "People say it's interesting but don't buy" | Offer the **AI Quick-Start** (low price, 2 hours) or a founding-client discount with a clear, fixed deliverable. |
| "I'm not technical enough to build X" | Start with no-code automation (n8n/Make) plus Claude, and use Claude Code for custom builds. For big builds, bring in a freelance developer and act as the project lead. |
| "I'm spending all my time building, not selling" | Block 2 hours of sales and outreach every morning *before* any build work. See the [weekly rhythm](plan/04-roadmap.md#weekly-operating-rhythm). |
