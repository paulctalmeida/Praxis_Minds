## Phase 0-1 – Foundation & launch (month 1)

**Follow [`START-HERE.md`](../START-HERE.md) step by step.**

**Exit criteria (move on when all are true):**
- [ ] Niche chosen; ICP written
- [ ] Company registered (or in progress), bank account, domain, email
- [ ] Website live with services and booking link
- [ ] 3 demos built and recorded
- [ ] Workshop delivered at least once (practice run counts)
- [ ] 50 contacts messaged; 10+ conversations held

## Phase 2 – First revenue (months 2-3)

**Goal:** 3–5 paying clients (founding clients included) and 2 case studies.

### Sales
- [ ] Follow up with every conversation from month 1 (most deals close on the 2nd–5th touch).
- [ ] Deliver **2–3 free/low-cost Lunch & Learn talks** for associations, chambers of commerce, or coworking spaces. Always end with an offer: "Free 20-minute AI check for the first 5 companies who book."
- [ ] Sign **2 referral partners** (accountant, IT provider, or web/marketing agency).
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn **3× per week** (see the [content plan](05-go-to-market.md#4-content-plan)).
- [ ] Target: **20 discovery calls → 8 proposals → 3–5 clients.**

### Delivery
- [ ] Deliver founding-client projects using the [delivery process](06-delivery-process.md).
- [ ] Measure **before and after** for every project (hours, errors, response time, cost). These numbers are your case studies.
- [ ] Ask for the testimonial **on the day the client says "wow"**, not weeks later.

### Business setup
- [ ] Contracts reviewed by a lawyer (MSA, SOW, NDA, DPA).
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance in place before the first build goes live.
- [ ] Invoicing and bookkeeping set up (accounting software + accountant).
- [ ] CRM pipeline in use (see [pipeline stages](05-go-to-market.md#7-sales-pipeline--crm)).

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 3 paying clients · ≥ 2 case studies (even small ones) · €3k+ revenue in a month · a repeatable discovery → proposal flow.

---

## Phase 3 – Traction & systems (months 4-6)

**Goal:** €6k–€8k/month revenue, 3 recurring retainers, and a delivery process you can repeat without reinventing anything.

### Sales & marketing
- [ ] Publish **case studies** on the website and LinkedIn (template: [case study](../templates/case-study-template.md)).
- [ ] Launch a **lead magnet**, e.g. "The 25 AI Quick Wins for [Niche] Companies" (PDF) or a free online AI readiness scorecard.
- [ ] Start a **monthly email newsletter** to contacts and workshop attendees.
- [ ] Hold a **free monthly webinar** ("AI in [Niche]: live demos").
- [ ] Ask every happy client for **2 introductions.**
- [ ] Offer the **AI Partner retainer** to every client finishing a project.

### Systems (make the business repeatable)
- [ ] Turn your best prompts into reusable Claude Projects/Skills (see the [Claude Playbook](07-claude-playbook.md)).
- [ ] Build a **component library** in this repo or a private one: reusable automation templates, app starters, dashboard templates, workshop modules.
- [ ] Standardize: proposal → SOW → kickoff → weekly update → handover → case study.
- [ ] Track **effective hourly rate** per project; drop or reprice the services that underperform.

### Decision gate (month 6)
| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| < 3 paying clients in total | Re-check niche and offer: talk to 10 non-buyers about why; consider a different niche with better access. |
| Clients buy small services but not builds | Strengthen proof: build a more impressive demo in the niche; offer a build with a performance guarantee. |
| One service sells far better than others | **Double down.** Make it your headline offer. |
| Fully booked | Raise prices 15–25%; start using a freelancer for overflow. |

**Exit criteria:** €6k+/month for 2 consecutive months · 3+ active retainers or maintenance plans · 5+ case studies/testimonials.

## Phase 4 – Growth & recurring revenue (months 7-12)

**Goal:** €10k–€15k/month, with €4k+/month recurring.

- [ ] **Productize the most-requested build** for your niche, e.g. "Invoice AI for distributors" as a fixed-price, fast-setup package, reused across clients.
- [ ] Launch **public workshops** (per-seat) quarterly; partner with a coworking space, business school, or association for the venue and promotion.
- [ ] Launch the **AI Champions Program** for mid-size clients.
- [ ] Bring in your **first subcontractor** (freelance developer, designer, or junior consultant) so you can sell more than you can personally deliver.
- [ ] Speak at 2–4 industry events in your niche.
- [ ] Explore **public funding**: many regions offer SME digitalization grants/vouchers that can pay for consulting and AI projects. Learn the programs and help clients apply (a strong selling point).
- [ ] Formalize your **AI governance offer** (policy pack + training records), since the EU AI Act makes AI literacy relevant for every company.
- [ ] Year-end review: revenue by service, profit by service, client satisfaction, what to stop doing.

**Exit criteria:** €10k+/month for 3 months · €4k+/month recurring · at least 1 productized offer · a dependable subcontractor bench.

## Phase 5 – Scale (year 2)

Choose a direction based on what year 1 taught you:

| Path | What it looks like | Best if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Boutique agency** | Team of 2–5, larger projects (€15k–€60k), multiple niches | Builds are profitable and you enjoy leading people |
| **Productized / software** | Turn the niche solution into a subscription product (SaaS) | Same problem keeps repeating across clients |
| **Training-led** | Public workshops, corporate academies, online courses, certification | Workshops get the best feedback and margins |
| **Hybrid** (most common) | Services fund the product; training feeds the pipeline | You want both stability and upside |

Year-2 milestones:
- [ ] First full-time hire (usually a builder/developer or a delivery consultant)
- [ ] €20k–€35k/month revenue
- [ ] 30%+ of revenue recurring
- [ ] Recognized brand in the chosen niche (speaking, partnerships, referrals)

## Weekly operating rhythm

A solo founder's week. Protect the **sales blocks** above everything else.

| Time | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08:30–10:30 | **Sales & outreach** | **Sales & outreach** | **Sales & outreach** | **Sales & outreach** | Weekly review & planning |
| 10:30–13:00 | Client delivery | Client delivery | Content creation (batch the week's posts) | Client delivery | Learning (new AI tools/features) |
| 14:00–17:30 | Client delivery | Discovery calls / meetings | Client delivery | Discovery calls / meetings | Admin, invoicing, follow-ups |

**Friday weekly review (30 minutes):**
1. Pipeline: who needs a follow-up? Which proposals are pending?
2. Delivery: are projects on track? Any client risks?
3. Numbers: revenue invoiced/collected; leads; calls.
4. Content: what's scheduled next week?
5. One improvement to the business this week.

**Monthly review (first Monday, 1 hour):** KPIs vs. targets, profit per service, what to stop/start/continue, update this roadmap.
