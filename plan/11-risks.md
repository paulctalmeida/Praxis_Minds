# 11 – Risks & Mitigation

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Offer too broad**: clients don't understand what you do | High | High | Lead with one flagship offer (Audit → Build) and one niche. Sell the other services to existing contacts. Review at the month-6 decision gate. |
| 2 | **Not enough clients early on** | Medium | High | Daily sales blocks; warm network first; Lunch & Learn talks; referral partners; founding-client discount; keep part-time income or a 4–6-month runway. |
| 3 | **Founder bottleneck**: building eats all your selling time | High | High | Protect morning sales blocks; productize; reuse components; subcontract from month 6–9. |
| 4 | **Underpricing** | High | Medium | Track effective hourly rate; raise prices after 3 case studies or when booked 4+ weeks ahead. |
| 5 | **Scope creep** | High | Medium | Written SOW with out-of-scope list; design sign-off before building; change-request process with a price. |
| 6 | **AI errors ("hallucinations") in deliverables** | Medium | High | Human review of everything; fact-check numbers and sources; human-in-the-loop steps in automations; accuracy testing before go-live; contract disclaimers. |
| 7 | **Data breach / confidentiality incident** | Low | Very high | Commercial AI terms; client-owned accounts; MFA and a password manager; data minimization; DPA; cyber insurance. |
| 8 | **Legal/regulatory**: building a high-risk AI system unknowingly | Low | High | Responsible AI checklist on every project; avoid employment/credit/biometric decision systems in years 1–2; lawyer review for edge cases. |
| 9 | **Commoditization**: clients think "we can do this ourselves with AI" | Medium | Medium | Sell outcomes, integration, and training, not "access to AI"; teach (it builds trust and leads to bigger work); niche expertise and case studies. |
| 10 | **Dependency on one AI vendor** (price, policy, or model changes) | Medium | Medium | Keep AI calls behind a small internal layer in your code; document prompts; test on new models before switching; be able to run Claude through the client's cloud (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure). |
| 11 | **Rapid technology change**: your solutions or skills date quickly | High | Medium | Weekly learning block; maintenance plans include model updates; frame this as a selling point ("we keep you current"). |
| 12 | **Late or missing payments** | Medium | Medium | Deposits; milestone billing; work stops when invoices are overdue (in contract); polite, firm follow-up schedule. |
| 13 | **Client dependency**: one client is more than 40% of revenue | Medium | High | Keep the pipeline active even when busy; aim for no client above 30% of revenue. |
| 14 | **Burnout** | Medium | High | Fixed weekly rhythm; say no to poor-fit clients; raise prices instead of working more hours; take time off between big projects. |
| 15 | **Subcontractor quality** | Medium | Medium | Start with a small paid test task; clear specs; you review everything before delivery; IP and confidentiality agreements. |

## Early-warning signals (check monthly)

- Fewer than **8 new conversations** a month → marketing problem
- Proposal win rate under **25%** → offer, pricing, or targeting problem
- Effective hourly rate under **€60** → pricing or scope problem
- More than **2 projects late** → capacity or process problem
- No new **case study** in 2 months → ask for testimonials sooner; measure results
