# 05 – Sales & Marketing (Go-to-Market)

## 1. The client journey (funnel)

```
AWARENESS            INTEREST               CONVERSATION           FIRST PURCHASE          EXPANSION
LinkedIn posts   →   Lunch & Learn talk  →  20-min discovery   →   Quick-Start / Audit  →  Build project
Referrals            Webinar / demo video   call                   Workshop                AI Partner retainer
Events               Lead magnet (PDF)      AI readiness check     Website / report        More departments
Partners             Newsletter                                                            Referrals
```

**The key insight:** AI services are bought on **trust**. Every marketing activity should either *show* expertise (demos, case studies, teaching) or *borrow* trust (referrals, partners, associations).

## 3. LinkedIn strategy

## 4. Content plan

Post **2–3×/week**. Rotate these 6 content types:

| Type | Example | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Demo** (video/GIF) | "Watch AI process 50 invoices in 2 minutes" | Show, don't tell |
| **Quick win / tip** | "3 prompts every operations manager should use" | Teach, build authority |
| **Case study / result** | "How a distributor saved 22 hours/week on order entry" | Proof |
| **Myth / opinion** | "AI won't replace your team. Untrained teams will fall behind." | Engagement, positioning |
| **Behind the scenes** | "What we learned in our 10th AI workshop" | Personality, trust |
| **Industry news, explained** | "What the EU AI Act's AI-literacy rule means for your company" | Relevance |

**Monthly content engine with Claude (about 2 hours per month):**

2. Draft all posts in your voice (the Project holds your style guide and past posts).
3. Edit, add personal stories and real numbers, and schedule (LinkedIn native scheduler or Buffer).
4. Turn the best post into a newsletter and a blog article.

**Lead magnet ideas**

## 5. Referral partnerships

| Partner | Why they refer | What you offer them |
|---|---|---|
| **Accountants / bookkeepers** | Their clients complain about admin overload | 10–15% referral fee, or free automation of *their* firm's processes as a showcase |
| **IT service providers / MSPs** | Clients ask them about AI; they don't do it | Referral fee + you don't compete on IT support; joint offers |
| **Web / marketing agencies** | Clients ask for AI features and automation | White-label delivery or referral fee; you refer website overflow to them |
| **Business consultants & coaches** | Their clients need implementation | Joint workshops; referral fees both ways |
| **Business associations, chambers of commerce** | Members want AI education | Free/discounted talks for members; co-branded workshops |
| **Coworking spaces / incubators / universities** | Startups need pitch decks, viability reports, websites | Workshops for members; member discount |
| **Banks / funding consultants** | Loan and grant applicants need viability reports and business plans | Referral arrangement, where permitted |

**How to approach a partner:** offer value first. "I'd love to give a free 45-minute AI session for your team or your clients," not "Please send me clients."

## 6. The sales process

```
1. First contact  →  2. Discovery call  →  3. (Optional) Quick-Start/Audit  →  4. Proposal  →  5. Follow-up  →  6. Close  →  7. Kickoff
   (message,          (20-30 min, free)                                          (within 48h)     (3-5 touches)
   talk, referral)
```

### Step 2: Discovery call (free, 20–30 minutes)
Use the [discovery call script](../templates/discovery-call-script.md). Goals:
- Understand the business, the pain, and its **cost** (hours, money, errors, lost sales).
- Learn who decides, the budget range, and the timing.
- Agree a clear next step (Quick-Start, Audit, or proposal).

**Golden rule:** talk 30% of the time, listen 70%. Ask "what does that cost you today?" at least once.

### Step 4: Proposal
- Send it **within 48 hours**, while interest is high.
- Use the [proposal template](../templates/proposal-template.md): their problem in their words → the solution → 3 options → timeline → investment → next steps.
- If possible, **present it live** (video call) instead of just emailing a PDF.
- Claude drafts it from your call notes in about 15 minutes (prompt in the [Claude Playbook](07-claude-playbook.md#4-prompt-library)).

### Step 5: Follow-up rhythm
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| +2 | "Any questions about the proposal?" |
| +5 | Share something useful: a relevant demo video or a mini case study |
| +10 | "Should I hold the [start date] slot for you?" |
| +20 | "Closing the loop. Is this still a priority, or should we revisit in [month]?" |

### Handling common objections
| Objection | Response approach |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive." | Go back to the cost of the problem: "You said this takes 15 hours a week, which is roughly €20k a year. The project pays for itself in about 3 months." Offer the smaller Essential option. |
| "We need to think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to feel confident?" Then address it. |
| "We'll do it ourselves with ChatGPT/Claude." | "Great, many teams start that way. We usually help when you want it to work reliably, connected to your systems, and used by the whole team." Offer a workshop. |
| "Is our data safe?" | Explain your data rules (commercial API terms, access controls, a DPA, no training on their data, EU hosting options). See [09 – Legal](09-legal-and-compliance.md). |
| "AI makes mistakes." | "Correct, which is why we design human checkpoints for important steps and measure accuracy before go-live." |
| "Not now." | Agree a date to reconnect, and add them to the newsletter. |

## 7. Sales pipeline & CRM

Use a free CRM (HubSpot Free, Pipedrive trial, Notion, or a spreadsheet). Pipeline stages:

| Stage | Definition | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lead | Name + reason to contact | Send a personal message |
| 2. Contacted | Message sent | Follow up in 3–5 days |
| 3. Discovery booked | Call scheduled | Prepare (website, LinkedIn, industry) |
| 4. Discovery done | Pain and budget understood | Send proposal within 48h |
| 5. Proposal sent | Waiting | Follow-up rhythm |
| 6. Won | Signed + deposit paid | Kickoff checklist |
| 7. Lost | Declined | Ask why; add to newsletter; revisit in 3–6 months |
| 8. Active client | In delivery | Weekly updates |
| 9. Recurring | Retainer/maintenance | Monthly review; look for expansion |

**Weekly pipeline math (month 3+ target):** 10 new leads → 4 discovery calls → 2 proposals → 1 client per week.

## 8. The Praxis Minds website

Build it yourself with Claude Code. It's your first portfolio piece for the website service.

### Sitemap
- **Home**
- **Services**: Find (Audit) · Build (Automation, Apps, Dashboards) · Teach (Workshops) · Business services (Websites, Reports, Decks)
- **Industries**: one page for your niche (e.g. "AI for Logistics")
- **Case studies**
- **Workshops**: upcoming public dates + in-company formats
- **About**: founder story, values, why "Praxis"
- **Resources/Blog**: articles, lead magnets
- **Contact / Book a call**: calendar booking (Calendly, Cal.com, or Google Calendar booking)
- Legal: privacy policy, cookie notice, terms

### Homepage structure
1. **Hero:** "AI, put into practice." Subhead: "We help [niche/SME] companies save time and grow with practical AI: automations, custom AI tools, dashboards, and team training." Button: **Book a free 20-minute AI check**.
2. **The problem:** 3 pain points in the client's words.
3. **How we work:** Find → Build → Teach (3 columns).
4. **Services overview** with "from €" prices (showing prices builds trust and filters out poor-fit leads).
5. **Demo video** (2 minutes).
6. **Results / case studies / testimonials** (add as soon as you have them).
7. **Workshops** teaser.
8. **About the founder:** photo and a short story.
9. **FAQ:** data security, cost, timeline, "do we need technical staff?"
10. **Final CTA:** book a call + lead magnet download.

### Must-haves
- Mobile-friendly, fast, clear CTA on every page
- Booking link and contact form
- Analytics (privacy-friendly, e.g. Plausible, or GA4 with a cookie banner)
- Basic SEO: titles, meta descriptions, one page per service and per niche
- An **AI assistant** on the site that answers questions about your services. It's also a live demo!
