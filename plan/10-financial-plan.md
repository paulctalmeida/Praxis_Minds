## 4. Break-even

**Business break-even** (covers running costs): ~€650/month. That's roughly **one Quick-Start + one small workshop a month**.

**Personal break-even** (covers running costs **and** the income you need to live):

```
Monthly target = running costs + (your required take-home pay ÷ (1 − tax & social-security rate))

Example: €650 + (€2,200 ÷ (1 − 0.35)) ≈ €650 + €3,385 ≈ €4,035/month
→ for example, 1 Audit (€2,500) + 1 workshop (€1,500) per month, or 2 websites.
```

Fill in your own numbers:

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Running costs per month | € |
| Required take-home pay per month | € |
| Estimated tax + social-security rate (ask your accountant) | % |
| **Personal break-even revenue per month** | **€** |

**Runway:** before going full-time, have **4–6 months of personal expenses** saved, or keep part-time income until revenue reaches personal break-even for 2–3 months in a row.

## 6. Cash-flow rules

1. **Deposits always:** 50% up front (100% under €2,000). See [payment terms](03-pricing.md#payment-terms).
2. **Invoice the same day** a milestone is reached.
3. **Tax reserve:** move **25–35%** of every payment (confirm the % with your accountant) into a separate tax savings account.
4. **Operating buffer:** build up **3 months of running costs** in the business account by month 6.
5. **Pay yourself a fixed monthly amount**, not "whatever's left," once revenue is stable.
6. **Chase late payments** at +3 days, +10 days, and +20 days, politely and firmly.

## 7. Funding

Praxis Minds needs very little capital, so **avoid debt or investors** at the start. Worth exploring:
- **Startup support programs** for new self-employed people/businesses (many countries offer grants, reduced social security, or unemployment-to-business schemes)
- **Regional digitalization programs** that subsidize SME consulting and technology adoption. These can pay for *your clients'* projects, making sales easier.
- **Incubators/accelerators** (free mentoring, a network, sometimes office space)
