# 01 – Vision & Strategy

## 1. Mission, vision, values

**Mission:** Help companies put AI into practice: fewer repetitive tasks, better decisions, and teams who know how to work with AI.

**Vision:** To be the go-to AI partner for small and medium businesses in our region and niche. We're known for delivering working results, not slide decks.

**The name:** *Praxis* (Greek) means *practice, action*: theory turned into real-world results. That is the brand promise: **we make AI work in your business, in practice.**

**Values**
1. **Results over hype.** Every engagement ends with something that works and a measurable outcome.
2. **Plain language.** No jargon. If a client can't explain what we built, we didn't finish the job.
3. **Teach, don't trap.** We train clients' teams so they're never dependent on us for the basics.
4. **Responsible AI.** We protect client data, keep humans in the loop for important decisions, and follow the law.
5. **Fixed scope, fixed price, on time.** No surprise invoices.

## 2. The problem we solve

Most small and medium businesses know AI matters but are stuck:

| What they say | What's really going on |
|---|---|
| "We know we should use AI, but where do we start?" | No clear map of where AI saves time or money in *their* processes |
| "Our people tried ChatGPT/Claude, but nothing changed." | Ad-hoc use, no training, no workflows or tools built around it |
| "We spend hours on [invoices / reports / emails / data entry]." | Repetitive manual work that's ideal for automation |
| "Our data lives in 5 different tools and nobody sees the full picture." | No dashboards or integrations between systems |
| "Big consultancies are too expensive, and freelancers disappear." | No trusted, affordable partner who both builds and supports |
| "We need a report / deck / website, but have no time." | Under-resourced for business documents and marketing |

## 3. Positioning

**Positioning statement:**
> For small and medium businesses that want real results from AI, **Praxis Minds** is the hands-on AI partner that **finds, builds, and teaches** practical AI solutions, at fixed prices and in weeks, not months. Unlike big consultancies, we build the solution ourselves. Unlike freelancers or software vendors, we start from your business problem and stay to support your team.

**Our three verbs (use them everywhere):** **Find → Build → Teach**
- **Find:** the AI Opportunity Audit identifies where AI pays off.
- **Build:** workflows, apps, dashboards, websites, and documents.
- **Teach:** workshops and training so teams adopt what we build.

## 4. Choosing your niche

Selling "AI for everyone" is hard. Selling "AI for logistics companies" (or accounting firms, or clinics) is much easier: your demos, case studies, and content all speak the client's language, and referrals spread inside the industry.

### Niche scorecard
Score each candidate industry from 1 to 5 on each criterion:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Can I name 10+ decision-makers in this industry personally or through one introduction? |
| **Pain** | Do they have obvious repetitive, document-heavy, or data-heavy work? |
| **Budget** | Can they spend €2k–€20k on a project without a board meeting? |
| **Knowledge** | Do I understand how this industry works (or can I learn it quickly)? |
| **Reachability** | Are there associations, events, or groups where they gather? |
| **Repeatability** | Will a solution for one company work for 10 others with small changes? |

Pick the highest total. Tie-breaker: **Access**.

### Strong candidate niches for an AI consultancy

| Niche | Typical AI wins |
|---|---|
| **Construction, engineering, industrial supply** | Quote/estimate drafting, document and spec extraction, project reporting dashboards, safety documentation |
| **Logistics & distribution** | Order/invoice processing, email triage, delivery and KPI dashboards, customer service replies |
| **Accounting & bookkeeping firms** | Document extraction, client communication drafting, report generation, onboarding checklists |
| **Real estate agencies** | Listing descriptions, lead follow-up, document checklists, market reports |
| **Clinics & health practices** (non-clinical tasks) | Scheduling communication, admin documents, FAQ assistants. *Avoid diagnostic uses.* |
| **Hospitality & tourism** | Review responses, multilingual content, booking communication, revenue dashboards |
| **Law firms** (admin side) | Document drafting templates, intake summaries, knowledge search. *Lawyer review is required.* |
| **Manufacturing** | Production dashboards, quality reports, SOP and manual assistants, supplier communication |
| **Professional services / agencies** | Proposal generation, reporting automation, knowledge assistants |

> Your niche is your **marketing focus**, not a restriction. You can still take good clients outside it.

## 5. Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

Fill this in once you choose your niche. Here is a starting version:

| Attribute | Ideal client |
|---|---|
| Company size | 10–250 employees; revenue €1M–€50M |
| Decision-maker | Owner, CEO, managing director, operations manager |
| Situation | Growing, overloaded team; lots of email, documents, spreadsheets; at least one "everyone hates this task" process |
| Tech maturity | Uses Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and some business software (ERP, CRM, accounting) |
| Mindset | Curious about AI, wants practical results, values training their people |
| Budget | €2k–€25k for a first project; open to a monthly retainer |
| Red flags | Wants "AI magic" with no clear problem; won't give access to data or people; haggles before understanding value; needs a fully regulated/high-risk system on day one |

## 6. Competitive landscape

| Competitor type | Their strength | Their weakness | How we win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big consultancies (Big 4, large IT integrators) | Brand, scale | Expensive, slow, focus on large enterprise | Fixed-price, fast, SME-friendly, the founder is personally involved |
| Software vendors adding "AI features" | Already installed | Generic; doesn't fit unique processes | We connect tools and build around *their* workflow |
| Freelancers (Upwork/Fiverr) | Cheap | No business analysis, no training, no support | Find → Build → Teach, plus an ongoing partnership |
| Marketing/web agencies adding "AI" | Existing clients | Mostly content, not operations | We cover operations, data, and training. **Partner with them** (referrals) |
| "Do it yourself" with ChatGPT/Claude | Free/cheap | No structure, inconsistent, no integration | We turn ad-hoc use into reliable workflows and trained teams |
| Online courses | Cheap, scalable | Generic, not applied to their business | Workshops tailored to their tools, roles, and real tasks |

**Our unfair advantages to build over time:**
1. **Niche case studies** ("we saved a logistics company 30 hours/week").
2. **A reusable component library**: every build makes the next one faster and cheaper to deliver.
3. **Relationships**: workshops create trust with whole teams, not just one buyer.

## 7. Brand

**Tagline options**
- **"AI, put into practice."** (recommended: it explains the name)
- "From AI ideas to working tools."
- "Practical AI for real businesses."
- "Find it. Build it. Teach it."

**Brand voice:** confident, practical, friendly, jargon-free. Prefer "We'll save your team 10 hours a week on invoices" to "We leverage cutting-edge generative AI paradigms."

**Visual identity (starter)**
- Colors: one deep, trustworthy color (navy, deep teal, or graphite) plus one energetic accent (amber, coral, or electric green).
- Typography: one clean sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Logo idea: a simple "PM" monogram, or a mark combining a brain/network motif with a forward arrow (thought → action).

**Elevator pitch (30 seconds)**
> "I run Praxis Minds. We help small and medium businesses put AI to work in practice. We find the tasks that eat your team's time, like invoices, reports, emails, or data entry, and we build simple AI tools and automations that handle them. Then we train your team to use them. Most clients see results in a few weeks, at a fixed price. What's the most repetitive task in your company right now?"
