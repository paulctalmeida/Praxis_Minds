# 03 – Pricing

> These prices are **starting assumptions** for a small/medium-business market in euros. Validate them in your first 10–20 sales conversations: if everyone says yes instantly, raise prices; if nobody does, check the offer and the target client before cutting the price.

## Pricing principles

1. **Fixed price per package, not hourly.** Clients buy outcomes. Fixed prices are easier to approve, and Claude's speed becomes *your* margin instead of a discount.
2. **Price on value, sanity-check on time.** If an automation saves 10 hours/week (≈ 500 hours/year × €25/hour = €12,500/year), a €5,000 price is an easy yes.
3. **Always offer 3 options** (good / better / best). Most clients pick the middle one.
4. **Make the first step small.** The Quick-Start and the Audit lower the risk of saying yes.
5. **Every project ends with a recurring offer** (maintenance, retainer, care plan).
6. **Know your floor:** target an *effective* rate of at least **€60–€120/hour** (price ÷ real hours spent). Track it per project.

| **Team AI Workshop** (90 minutes to half-day, up to 20 people) | **€800–€2,500** | Lead generator for audits and builds |

### Founding Client Program
For your **first 3–5 clients only**:
- **30–50% discount** on the Audit or the first build.
- **In exchange:** a written testimonial, a case study with real numbers (anonymized if needed), permission to use the logo, and 2 referrals if they're happy.
- Put the deadline and limit in writing ("Founding client pricing is available for 5 companies until [date]").

## The "3 options" proposal structure

Every proposal offers three levels. Example for a logistics company's invoice-processing project:

| | **Essential** | **Recommended** | **Complete** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice extraction to spreadsheet | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Direct posting to accounting/ERP | – | ✅ | ✅ |
| Exception handling + approval step | – | ✅ | ✅ |
| Team training (1 hour) | – | ✅ | ✅ |
| Management dashboard of invoice volumes/costs | – | – | ✅ |
| 3 months of maintenance included | – | – | ✅ |
| **Price** | **€2,500** | **€4,800** | **€7,900** |

## Payment terms

| Project size | Terms |
|---|---|
| Under €2,000 | 100% up front (or card payment at booking) |
| €2,000–€10,000 | 50% at signing, 50% at delivery |
| Over €10,000 | 40% signing / 30% midpoint milestone / 30% delivery |
| Retainers & care plans | Monthly in advance, 3-month minimum, then month-to-month (30 days' notice) |

- Payment due within **14 days** of invoice.
- Work starts only after the deposit is received.
- Clearly state what's **out of scope** and the **change request rate** (e.g. €80–€100/hour or a quoted fixed price).
- Revisions: **2 rounds included** for documents, websites, and designs.

---

## When and how to raise prices

- After **3 case studies** in a service, raise its price by 15–25%.
- When you're booked **4+ weeks ahead**, raise prices for new clients.
- Review the rate card **every 6 months**.
- Grandfather existing retainer clients for 6–12 months, then align them.
