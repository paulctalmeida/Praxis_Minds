# Workshop Curriculum

All workshops follow the same principles:
- **70% hands-on, 30% talk.** People learn AI by using it on their own tasks.
- **Tailored examples** from the client's industry, tools, and roles (prompt 4.9 in the [Claude Playbook](../plan/07-claude-playbook.md#49-tailor-a-workshop-to-a-client)).
- **Safe-use rules** in every session (data, accuracy, transparency).
- **Evidence for AI literacy:** attendance list, short quiz, certificate. Clients can use these to document AI-literacy measures under the EU AI Act.

**Standard materials per workshop:** slides · hands-on workbook · role-specific prompt cheat-sheet · quiz · certificate of attendance · feedback form · recording (remote sessions, with consent).

**Before every in-company workshop:** send a 5-question pre-survey (roles, tools used, AI experience, top 3 time-consuming tasks, concerns).

## Workshop 1 – AI Fundamentals for Teams
**Format:** 90 minutes (intro) or half-day (3.5 hours, with more practice) · up to 20 people · on-site or remote
**Audience:** all employees, no technical background needed
**Outcome:** everyone can use an AI assistant safely and effectively for 3+ tasks in their job.

| Time (half-day) | Module | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Welcome & AI demystified** | What generative AI is (and isn't) in plain language; live demo with their own industry examples; what it means for their jobs |
| 0:20–0:50 | **What AI is good and bad at** | Strengths (writing, summarizing, analysing, brainstorming), limits (errors/"hallucinations", out-of-date knowledge, bias); exercise: spot the mistake |
| 0:50–1:40 | **Prompting that works** | The 5-part prompt: role, context, task, format, examples; iterating and giving feedback; exercise: improve 3 bad prompts |
| 1:40–1:55 | *Break* | |
| 1:55–2:50 | **Hands-on: your own tasks** | Each person works on their pre-survey tasks: emails, summaries, reports, analysis, ideas; share wins |
| 2:50–3:15 | **Safe & responsible use** | Company data rules (what never goes into AI tools), checking outputs, transparency with customers, copyright basics, the EU AI Act in 5 minutes |
| 3:15–3:30 | **Next steps & quiz** | Personal action plan (3 tasks to use AI for this week), quiz, feedback |

*90-minute version:* modules 1, 3 (shortened), one hands-on exercise, safe use, action plan.

## Workshop 2 – AI for Your Role (track options)
**Format:** half-day per track · up to 15 people from the same function

| Track | Hands-on use cases |
|---|---|
| **Sales & marketing** | Prospect research, personalized outreach, proposals, social content calendar, customer insights from reviews |
| **Admin & operations** | Email handling, document summaries, SOP writing, meeting notes to action items, spreadsheet formulas and analysis |
| **Finance** | Data analysis in spreadsheets, report commentary, variance explanations, policy Q&A, checking documents (with care on accuracy) |
| **HR** | Job descriptions, onboarding materials, policy drafts, internal communications, training content. *Includes the limits: no AI-based candidate ranking or employee evaluation without legal review.* |
| **Customer service** | Reply drafting, knowledge base articles, tone adjustment, multilingual responses, FAQ creation |
| **Management** | Decision briefs, strategy brainstorming, market research, presentation building, analysing reports |

## Workshop 3 – Build Your First AI Workflow
**Format:** full day (6 hours) · up to 12 people · laptops required
**Audience:** "power users" and process owners
**Outcome:** each participant leaves with one working AI-assisted workflow for their job.

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Pick your process | Map one repetitive task; define input → steps → output; estimate time saved |
| 2. Projects & instructions | Set up a Claude Project (or equivalent) with context, instructions, and reference files |
| 3. Templates & reusable prompts | Turn the task into a reusable prompt/template; test on 5 real examples |
| 4. Files & data | Working with documents, spreadsheets, and creating files (reports, slides) |
| 5. Intro to automation | How no-code tools (n8n/Make/Power Automate) connect AI to email, forms, and systems; live demo |
| 6. Show & tell | Each participant demos their workflow; the group gives feedback; plan to roll it out |

## Workshop 4 – AI for Leaders
**Format:** half-day · 4–12 executives/managers
**Outcome:** a shared AI vision, a shortlist of priority use cases, and governance decisions.

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. The state of AI (business view) | What's realistic now; examples from their industry; costs and ROI |
| 2. Opportunity mapping | Facilitated exercise: list processes → score impact vs. effort → pick top 5 |
| 3. Risks & governance | Data protection, accuracy, EU AI Act basics (AI literacy, transparency, high-risk areas), AI policy essentials |
| 4. People & change | Communicating with staff, training plan, AI champions, job redesign |
| 5. 90-day plan | Owners, budget, first pilots, success metrics |

*Natural follow-up:* AI Opportunity Audit + AI Policy & Governance Pack.

## AI Champions Program (6 weeks)
**Format:** weekly 2-hour session + office hours · 5–15 participants · kickoff and demo day with management

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff: AI foundations + each champion chooses one real process to improve |
| 2 | Advanced prompting, Projects, and reusable templates |
| 3 | Working with documents, data, and file outputs |
| 4 | Automation basics: connecting AI to everyday tools |
| 5 | Testing, measuring time saved, and safe-use review |
| 6 | **Demo day:** each champion presents their workflow and results to management |

**Deliverables:** 5–15 documented workflows with measured time savings, an internal prompt library, trained champions who support colleagues, and AI-literacy records.
