# AI Readiness Assessment

Use it as:
- a **lead magnet** (online quiz with an instant score, e.g. Google Forms, Typeform, or a Claude-built web app),
- a **pre-workshop survey**, or
- **part of the AI Opportunity Audit** (sent to the leadership team and staff).

Score each statement from **1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree)**.

---

## Section A: Strategy & leadership
1. Leadership sees AI as a priority for the next 12 months.
2. We have identified specific business problems where AI could help.
3. There is a person or team responsible for AI/digital initiatives.
4. We have (or are willing to allocate) budget for AI projects.

## Section B: Processes
5. Our main processes are documented (written procedures or checklists).
6. We know how much time our team spends on repetitive tasks.
7. A significant part of our work involves documents, emails, or data entry.
8. We have processes that follow clear rules and repeat frequently.

## Section C: Data & systems
9. Our important data is stored digitally (not mainly on paper).
10. Our key systems (ERP, CRM, accounting) can export data or connect to other tools.
11. Our data is reasonably clean and consistent.
12. We know where our sensitive data (customer, employee, financial) is stored.

## Section D: People & skills
13. Most employees are comfortable with digital tools.
14. Some employees already use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.) at work.
15. Employees have received training on how to use AI tools effectively and safely.
16. The team is open to changing how they work.

## Section E: Governance & risk
17. We have rules about which data can be entered into AI tools.
18. We have an approved list of AI tools.
19. We keep records of AI training for staff (relevant to EU AI Act AI-literacy duties).
20. We know which AI uses would be high-risk or sensitive for our business.

---

## Open questions
- Which 3 tasks in your job take the most time and feel repetitive?
- If you had 5 extra hours per week, what would you spend them on?
- What worries you most about using AI at work?
- Which AI tools, if any, do you use today, and for what?

---

## Scoring

Total score = sum of 20 answers (20–100).

| Score | Level | What it means | Recommended next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| **20–40** | 🌱 **Explorer** | Early stage: AI isn't yet part of how you work | AI Fundamentals workshop + AI Quick-Start |
| **41–60** | 🚀 **Experimenter** | Some use, but ad hoc; no structure or integration | AI Opportunity Audit + AI policy pack |
| **61–80** | ⚙️ **Implementer** | Good foundations, ready for real automation | Workflow automation / AI app build |
| **81–100** | 🏆 **Leader** | Mature: ready to scale AI across the business | AI Partner retainer, advanced builds, AI Champions program |

Also report the **score per section** (A–E). The weakest section shows where to start: e.g. low **D** → training first; low **C** → data/integration work first; low **E** → policy pack.
