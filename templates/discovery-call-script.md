# Discovery Call Script (20–30 minutes)

**Goal:** understand the problem and its cost, qualify the client (fit, budget, timing, decision-maker), and agree a clear next step.
**Rule:** listen 70%, talk 30%. Take notes, or record with permission and let Claude summarize afterwards.

---

## Before the call (10 minutes)
- [ ] Run prompt 4.2 "Prepare for a discovery call" from the [Claude Playbook](../plan/07-claude-playbook.md#42-prepare-for-a-discovery-call)
- [ ] Check their website, LinkedIn, and recent news
- [ ] Pick the 1 demo most relevant to them
- [ ] Have your calendar open to book the next step

---

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)
> "Thanks for making the time, [Name]. The idea for today is that I learn about your business and where you're losing time, and if I see a good fit, I'll suggest a next step. If not, I'll still try to give you one or two useful ideas. Sound good?"

## 2. The business (3 minutes)
- "Tell me briefly about [Company]: what you do, team size, and where you're heading this year."
- "Which departments or roles are the busiest right now?"

## 3. The pain (8–10 minutes). This is the most important part.
- "What tasks take the most time that you feel shouldn't?"
- "Walk me through how [that task] happens today, step by step."
- "How often does it happen? How many people are involved? How long does it take?"
- "What goes wrong? Errors, delays, unhappy customers?"
- **"What does that cost you today, in hours or money?"** ← always ask this
- "What happens if nothing changes in the next 12 months?"
- "Have you tried anything already? Tools, AI, hiring?"

## 4. AI experience & readiness (3 minutes)
- "Is your team using AI tools today? Which ones, and how?"
- "Any concerns about AI: data, quality, people's jobs?"
- "What systems do you use? (ERP, CRM, accounting, email, Microsoft/Google)"

## 5. Qualification (3 minutes)
- **Decision:** "Besides you, who else would be involved in deciding on a project like this?"
- **Timing:** "When would you ideally like to see this solved?"
- **Budget:** "Projects like this typically range from €[X] to €[Y], depending on scope. Is that in the range you'd consider?"

## 6. Mini demo (optional, 3 minutes)
Show the most relevant demo: "Here's something similar we built…"

## 7. Next step (2 minutes)
Pick one:
- **Clear problem, clear budget** → "I'll send you a proposal with 3 options by [day]. Can we book 30 minutes on [date] to walk through it?"
- **Several possible problems** → "The best next step is the AI Opportunity Audit [or Quick-Start]. We map your processes and give you a ranked list of opportunities with expected savings."
- **Team needs to learn first** → propose a workshop.
- **Not a fit / not now** → give 1–2 free tips, offer the newsletter, and agree a date to reconnect.

> "Before we finish: is there anything that would stop you from moving forward if the proposal looks right?"
