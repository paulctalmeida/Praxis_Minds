# Case Study Template

Case studies are your **most powerful sales asset**. Write one for every project with a measurable result (with client permission; anonymize if needed: "a 60-person logistics company in [region]").

Use prompt 4.15 in the [Claude Playbook](../plan/07-claude-playbook.md#415-case-study) to draft it from your project data.
