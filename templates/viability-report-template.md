# Viability / Feasibility Report Template

> Use with the "Praxis – Reports" Claude Project and prompt 4.11 in the [Claude Playbook](../plan/07-claude-playbook.md#411-viability-report).
> **Deliverables:** report (Word/PDF, 20–40 pages) + financial model (Excel) + summary presentation (8–12 slides).
> **Quality rule:** every fact has a source and date; every assumption is labelled; every projection has a method.

---

## Client intake checklist (collect before starting)
- [ ] Project description: what, where, when, for whom
- [ ] Founder/company background and experience
- [ ] Purpose of the report: internal decision, bank loan, investors, public funding (check required format)
- [ ] Investment budget and financing plan (equity, loans, grants)
- [ ] Location(s) and key operational facts (size, capacity, staff, opening hours)
- [ ] Known prices, quotes, rents, salaries, supplier costs
- [ ] Target customers and expected pricing
- [ ] Known competitors
- [ ] Deadline and required language/format

## 1. Executive summary (1–2 pages, written last)
- The project in 3 sentences
- Market opportunity (key numbers)
- Investment required and financing
- Key financial results (base case): revenue year 3, break-even point, payback, NPV/IRR
- Main risks and how they're mitigated
- **Recommendation:** Viable / viable with conditions / not viable, and why

## Quality checklist before delivery
- [ ] Every statistic has a source + date; no unsourced numbers
- [ ] Assumptions are realistic and checked against at least 2 sources or client quotes
- [ ] Financial model formulas checked (totals, links between sheets, no hard-coded overrides)
- [ ] Executive summary numbers match the model
- [ ] Scenarios and sensitivity included
- [ ] Spelling, formatting, consistent units and currency
- [ ] Disclaimer included
- [ ] If for a bank or a public funding program: required format and sections followed; accountant sign-off if required
