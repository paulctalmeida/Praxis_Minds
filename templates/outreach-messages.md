# Outreach Messages

**Rules:** personal > generic · short > long · ask for a conversation, not a sale · always mention something specific about *them* · follow up 2–3 times, politely · respect opt-outs (and anti-spam/GDPR rules for cold email).

Personalize each message with Claude (prompt 4.14 in the [Claude Playbook](../plan/07-claude-playbook.md#414-personalized-outreach-message)), but **read and edit every message before sending**.

---

## 1. Warm network: launch message (people who know you)

> Hi [Name], I hope you're well!
>
> Quick news: I've started **Praxis Minds**, a company that helps businesses put AI to work in practice: automating repetitive tasks (invoices, emails, reports), building simple AI tools, and training teams to use AI well.
>
> I'm looking for 3–5 **founding clients** to work with at a reduced rate in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial.
>
> Not asking you to buy anything, but I'd love 20 minutes to hear what's eating your team's time at [Company]. Even if it's not a fit, I'll share a couple of ideas you can use straight away. Coffee or a call next week?

## 2. Warm network: ask for introductions

> Hi [Name], I've just launched Praxis Minds (practical AI for businesses: automation, AI tools, and team training).
>
> Who's the one business owner or manager you know who's always complaining about admin, paperwork, or not having enough time? I'd be grateful for an introduction, and I promise to be useful to them, not pushy.

## 3. LinkedIn connection note (≤ 300 characters)

> Hi [Name], I saw your post about [topic] / I work with [niche] companies on practical AI (automating admin, reports, customer emails). I'd be glad to connect and share ideas.

## 4. LinkedIn follow-up after connecting

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! Quick question: at [Company], which task takes up your team's time and would you love to see automated? I've been helping [niche] companies with things like [invoice processing / quote drafting / weekly reporting]. Happy to share a 2-minute demo if useful. No strings attached.

## 5. Cold email (niche-specific, personalized)

**Subject:** [Company] + [specific process, e.g. "order entry"]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I noticed [specific observation: e.g. "you've opened two new warehouses this year"]. Growth like that usually means more [orders/invoices/emails] for the admin team.
>
> At Praxis Minds we help [niche] companies automate that kind of work with AI. A recent example: [result, e.g. "a distributor cut order-entry time from 15 to 3 hours a week"].
>
> Would a 20-minute call be useful to see if something similar could work at [Company]? Here's a 2-minute demo: [link].
>
> Best,
> [Name] · Praxis Minds · [phone] · [website]
>
> *If this isn't relevant, just reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.*

**Follow-up 1 (+4 days):**
> Hi [Name], just floating this back up. Happy to send a short video showing how this works with documents like yours instead, if that's easier.

**Follow-up 2 (+10 days):**
> Hi [Name], last note from me. If AI for [process] becomes a priority later this year, I'm happy to help. Meanwhile, here's our free [checklist "25 AI quick wins for [niche]"]: [link]. All the best!

## 6. Referral partner approach (accountant / IT provider / agency)

> Hi [Name],
>
> I run Praxis Minds. We help small and medium businesses use AI in practice: automating admin work and training teams. Many of your clients probably ask you about AI, and it's likely not something you want to spend your time on.
>
> I'd love to offer a free 45-minute "AI in practice" session for your team or your clients. No sales pitch, just useful ideas. If it goes well, we can talk about referring work to each other (with a referral fee for anything that comes from you).
>
> Would you be open to a short coffee to explore it?

## 7. Business association / chamber of commerce: talk offer

> Hello [Name],
>
> I'd like to offer [Association]'s members a free 45-minute session: **"AI in Practice: 10 Ways [Niche/Local] Businesses Are Saving Time Right Now."** Live demos, no jargon, and practical takeaways people can use the next day, including what the new EU AI-literacy rules mean for employers.
>
> I can run it as a breakfast/lunch session or online. Could we find a date in [month]?

## 8. After a workshop or talk

> Hi [Name], thanks for joining [workshop/talk] yesterday! As promised, here are the slides and the prompt cheat-sheet: [link].
>
> You mentioned [their specific challenge]. If you'd like, I can do a free 20-minute AI check for [Company] to look at that specifically. Here's my calendar: [link].

## 10. Asking a happy client for a testimonial and referrals

> Hi [Name], it's been great to see [result, e.g. "the team processing invoices in a fraction of the time"]. Two small favours:
> 1. Would you write 2–3 sentences about your experience working with us? (Happy to draft something from our conversations for you to edit.)
> 2. Who are 1–2 people you know who'd benefit from something similar? An introduction would mean a lot.
>
> Thank you!
