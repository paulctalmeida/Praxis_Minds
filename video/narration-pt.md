# Narração em português (pt-PT) para o filme

Fifteen short voice clips for `praxis-minds-film-pt.mp4`, one per scene. Each clip has a start time and a maximum length, so the voice stays in step with the picture. Generate one audio file per clip (named `pt-01.mp3` to `pt-15.mp3`) and the voice track can be placed clip by clip.

The "Texto para o ElevenLabs" column is what to paste into ElevenLabs. Its odd spellings are deliberate, so the voice pronounces the words correctly:
- **"i-á"** for IA. Written "IA", the voice can read it as the verb "ia".
- **"é érre pê"** for ERP.
- **"Práxis Maindz"** for Praxis Minds. Without the accent the stress falls on the last syllable, and "Maindz" keeps the English "Minds".

The numbers are written out in words so they are read correctly. They match the examples on screen: 13 h 30 min, 10 h 45 min, 307 and 843 hours.

## ElevenLabs settings
- **Voice:** in the Voice Library, filter by *Portuguese* and pick a voice with a **European (Portugal)** accent. Many Portuguese voices are Brazilian, so listen to clip 01 before generating the rest.
- **Model:** Eleven Multilingual v2 (or v3).
- **Settings:** stability about 50%, similarity about 75%, style low, speed 1.0.
- **Format:** MP3 44.1 kHz (128 kbps or higher), or WAV.
- **Length:** if a clip comes out longer than its "Máx.", regenerate it at speed 1.05 to 1.1, or send it as it is. It can be sped up by a few percent without anyone noticing.
