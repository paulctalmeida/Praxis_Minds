# Praxis Minds

> **AI, put into practice.**
> An AI consultancy that helps companies work faster and solve real problems with AI — through custom apps, automated workflows, dashboards, hands-on training, and AI-powered business services.

This repository is the **operating manual** for building Praxis Minds: the strategy, the step-by-step launch plan, the service catalog, pricing, sales process, delivery process, legal checklist, financial model, and ready-to-use templates.

## One-page summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **What we sell** | Practical AI results: automated workflows, custom AI apps, dashboards, AI training, plus fast AI-assisted services (websites, viability reports, pitch decks, marketing content). |
| **Who we sell to** | Small and medium businesses (10–250 employees) in 1–2 chosen industries, starting with the founder's own network. |
| **Why us** | We don't just advise, we **build and train**. Fixed prices, fast delivery, and every engagement ends with something working plus a team that knows how to use it. |
| **How we get clients** | Warm network → free "AI Lunch & Learn" talks → paid **AI Opportunity Audit** → implementation project → monthly **AI Partner** retainer. |
| **Entry offer** | **AI Opportunity Audit** (€1,500–€3,500): we map processes, find the top 5 AI wins, and deliver a prioritized roadmap. Fully credited toward a build if they continue. |
| **Cash-flow services** | Websites, viability reports, pitch decks, and workshops. These are quick to deliver with Claude and fund the business while larger projects ramp up. |
| **Recurring revenue** | AI Partner retainer, app maintenance/hosting, website care plans, marketing content retainers. |
| **Year-1 target (base case)** | ~€100k revenue, 10+ case studies, ~€4k/month recurring revenue by month 12. See [financial plan](plan/10-financial-plan.md). |
| **Startup cost** | Low: roughly €1,500–€3,500 one-time plus €300–€700/month in running costs. No office needed. |

## The model in one picture

```
 FREE                 ENTRY                 CORE                       RECURRING
 ─────────────        ─────────────         ─────────────────          ─────────────────
 LinkedIn content  →  AI Quick-Start    →   Workflow automation   →    AI Partner retainer
 Lunch & Learn        AI Opportunity        Custom AI apps             Maintenance & hosting
 Free AI checklist    Audit                 Dashboards/integrations    Website care plans
 Webinars             Intro workshop        Team training programs     Content retainers
                      Website / report /
                      pitch deck
```

Each step builds trust for the next one. The plan focuses on moving clients **left to right**.
